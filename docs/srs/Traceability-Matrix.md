## Future Scope Excluded from Traceability

- Stored scheduling preferences and lecturer availability.
- Dedicated lecturer and expanded university-administration journeys.
- Private university workspaces.
- External calendar providers and live feeds.
- Analytics, simulation, master scheduling, and persisted audit records.
